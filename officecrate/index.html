The details

Questions, answered.

Everything you need to know before your first box.

How is this different from Stitch Fix?

OfficeCrate is office-only curation with a keep-everything model and preview & swap. It's built around dress codes, not weekends.

What if something doesn't fit?

Each box includes one free size exchange, and your stylist records the adjustment for your next box.

Do I keep everything?

Yes. Everything in your box is included in one flat price per box.

Is this a subscription?

No. Your first box is a one-time purchase. If you opt in to quarterly refreshes, you approve each preview before you are charged, and you can turn refreshes off online anytime with no phone call.

When does my box ship?

We source each founding box after you approve your preview, so plan on about 2–3 weeks from approval to your door. You will get tracking as soon as it ships.

Who is OfficeCrate for?

OfficeCrate serves men and women across business formal, business casual, and smart casual dress codes.

Do you carry women’s sizing?

Yes. The women’s line is planned in sizes XS–XL, with fit preferences captured in your style quiz.
