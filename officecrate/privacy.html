Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

When you join the waitlist, we collect your name, your email address, the line you're interested in, your office dress code, the box you'd want, and when you'd buy. We also record which link brought you to the page (for example, a campaign tag in the URL) and the referring site.

How we use it

  • To invite you to buy a founding box and to send launch updates you opted into.
  • To understand which products, sizes, and channels to prioritize.

We don't sell your information or share it with advertisers. Our providers, such as Google Workspace for storage and our email and payment tools, process it on our behalf.

Your choices

Every email includes an unsubscribe link. To see, correct, or delete your information, email us at hello@officecrate.com and we'll take care of it.

Retention

We keep waitlist data until launch plus 12 months, or until you ask us to delete it, whichever comes first.
